    Frequently Asked Questions

    Everything you need to know about vehicle rentals, private chauffeur tours, and island travel.

    What are the age requirements for renting a vehicle?

    You must be at least 18 years old to rent a scooter/bike, and 21 years old to rent a car in Sri Lanka. These requirements align with local Department of Motor Traffic regulations.

    What documents do I need to rent a scooter or car in Sri Lanka?

    To rent a scooter or motorbike, you need a valid driver's license issued in Sri Lanka, or an International Driving Permit (IDP) 1949/1968 convention endorsed for motorcycle category (Class A). We can assist you with AA Ceylon verification if needed. A copy of your passport is required for contract verification.

    Do you require a security deposit?

    Yes, a refundable security deposit is standard for all rentals, payable at vehicle handover (cash or card) and promptly refunded when the vehicle is returned in good condition. Scooters typically require $50 / Rs. 15,000.

    What insurance coverage do you provide?

    All vehicles carry comprehensive third-party and accidental insurance from Sri Lanka Insurance Corporation. Valid insurance certificates and revenue licenses are housed in the vehicle's compartment at all times.

    Are there any hidden charges or fees?

    No. We maintain strict transparent pricing. Your rental quotation includes two sanitized helmets, basic insurance, local taxes, phone holder, and free delivery in Matara, Polhena, and Mirissa.

    How are the scooters and cars maintained?

    Every vehicle undergoes a 12-point mechanical check before every handover: brake pads, tire pressure and tread, lights, fluid levels, and battery condition. Regular oil servicing is completed every 1,500 km.

    How do I return the vehicle?

    On your scheduled checkout date, our team will meet you at your hotel or chosen location for a brief 3-minute handover check, after which your deposit is returned on the spot.

    How can I pay for the rental?

    We accept cash (LKR, USD, EUR, GBP), major debit/credit cards (Visa, Mastercard), and direct local bank transfer.

    Are helmets provided for scooter rentals?

    Yes. Two clean, sanitized, safety-certified helmets (rider and pillion) are included free of charge with every scooter or motorbike rental.

    What happens if my vehicle breaks down?

    Call or WhatsApp our 24/7 helpline immediately (+94 70 761 5516). We provide prompt mobile mechanics across the South Coast or deliver an immediate replacement vehicle if required.

    What's included in a private tour package?

    Our private tours include an experienced English-speaking licensed chauffeur, climate-controlled premium vehicle, all petrol/diesel costs, expressway highway tolls, parking fees, and driver meals/lodging. Door-to-door hotel transfers are fully covered.

    Can I customize my private tour itinerary?

    100% yes! We specialize in bespoke private circuits. Whether you wish to spend extra time in Ella's tea mountains, detour for a Yala morning safari, or linger in Galle Fort, we design the route entirely around your timeline.

    Do you offer instant online booking with card payment?

    Yes! While RIDE X HUB offers rapid WhatsApp dispatch, our premium sister brand Viungo Tours offers online pre-booking and card payments (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) via Stripe for curated itineraries and luxury vehicles.

    Can you help with airport VIP handling and hotel bookings?

    Yes. In partnership with Royal Aviation Lanka (Pvt) Ltd — CAASL-approved ground handler — we coordinate end-to-end arrival tarmac assistance, priority customs fast-tracking, luxury transfers, and curated boutique hotel reservations.

    What emergency numbers should I know in Sri Lanka?

    Ride & Tour 24/7 Helpline: +94 70 761 5516
    Police Emergency: 119
    Ambulance (Suwasariya 24/7 Free): 1990
    Tourist Police (National Hotline): +94 11 242 1052

    What's the best time to visit Sri Lanka's South Coast?

    November through April offers calm seas, sunny blue skies, and optimal surf/snorkel conditions on the South Coast (Matara, Mirissa, Hirikatiya, Weligama). The Hill Country (Ella, Nuwara Eliya) remains fresh and inviting year-round.
